    Limits and things to know

    Static JPEG / PNG, up to 16 million pixels per image and 8,192 px on either edge. Animated images, existing PDFs, OCR, encryption, signatures, and printing are not supported.

    Transparent areas become white. Photos are re-encoded as JPEG, which may slightly change image quality. Small images may be enlarged without gaining detail. Browser memory or encoding limits can also stop processing.

    Add the JPEG or PNG images to combine. You can adjust their order next.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will images be cropped or enlarged?

    Images are scaled to fit and centered without cropping. Unused page area remains white. Small images may be enlarged, without gaining detail.

    Does it use the camera’s orientation?

    It uses supported EXIF orientation first, then your extra rotation. A decoded size mismatch stops export. Check mirrors and reading direction in the photo preview.

    Will the PDF contain filenames or GPS data?

    Source filenames and photo metadata are not added. The PDF contains re-encoded page images. Names, addresses, or other details visible in the image remain visible; check them yourself.
